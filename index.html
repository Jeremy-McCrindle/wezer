Wezer's Blurbs

About WezTerm:

The goal of our project is to gain an overall view of the architecture of a large software system. We will be looking at the strengths and limitations of the architecture as well as proposing our own enhancement to the current system.

This year's Software Architecture topic is WezTerm:

"WezTerm is a GPU-accelerated, cross-platform terminal emulator and multiplexer written in Rust, designed for developers who want deep, programmable control over their terminal environment.""

"Unlike traditional terminal applications that rely on graphical menus or static JSON/YAML files for settings, WezTerm is configured entirely using the Lua scripting language ("code-as-configuration").""
